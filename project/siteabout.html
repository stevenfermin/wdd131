History

In 1995, Steven Fermin was trying to buy a house, but when he was looking the catalog of the company, was confused and disappointed for the time that we waited to just review the type of cars that he wanted because he was looking for a Sedan, after that experience, he proposed himself to create a way to help the customer to find a better service, being faster and efficient. 1 year later, Steven thought in creating a Web page where the customer can even look everywhere for new options and also have a contact to receive more details about the new car, and that's how CarStock went to life.
